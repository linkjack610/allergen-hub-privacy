Support

For help with Allergen Hub, contact support@allergenhub.store.

Include these details to speed up support

  • device model
  • Android version
  • app version
  • what you were trying to do
  • what happened instead
  • whether the issue is repeatable

Response times

Support requests are usually answered within 2–3 business days.

For feedback, feature suggestions, or bug reports, email support@allergenhub.store.

Important

Allergen Hub assists with reviewing ingredient information and maintaining records. It does not replace packaging checks, supplier documentation, regulatory guidance, or food safety procedures.

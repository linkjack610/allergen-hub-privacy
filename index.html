Built for food businesses

Review ingredient labels and keep allergen records in one reliable place.

Allergen Hub is a paid Android app for cafés, bakeries, caterers, delis, restaurants, and food retailers. It helps teams review label text, identify major allergens, and store product records on-device with an offline-first workflow.

Get it on Google Play Contact support

Offline-first operation

Product records are stored locally so staff can keep working even in low-connectivity environments.

Structured records

Keep product names, ingredient text, allergen indicators, and label references in a consistent format for quick lookup.

Assisted review workflow

OCR and allergen detection assist review, while your team stays in control of final verification decisions.

How teams typically use Allergen Hub

  • Capture or upload ingredient label images
  • Review extracted ingredient text and allergen highlights
  • Correct records where needed and keep audit-ready consistency
  • Use the same process across shared business devices

Important compliance note

Allergen Hub is an assistance tool. It does not replace packaging checks, supplier documentation, staff training, food safety controls, or legal obligations.

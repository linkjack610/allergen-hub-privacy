Offline-first food business tool

Review food labels and keep allergen records in one place.

Allergen Hub is an offline-first app for food businesses that need a faster way to review ingredient labels, identify allergens, and keep structured product records on-device.

Get it on Google Play Contact support

Built for real use

Designed for cafés, bakeries, sweet shops, delis, caterers, restaurants, food retailers, and other small food businesses.

Offline-first

Product records are stored locally on-device for fast lookup without relying on constant connectivity.

Controlled workflow

Review OCR results, correct ingredient text when needed, and use verification controls on shared business devices.

What the app does

  • Scan or upload ingredient labels
  • Extract ingredient text using on-device OCR
  • Identify major allergens from ingredient information
  • Store structured product records for future lookup
  • Support manual review and verification workflows

Important

Allergen Hub assists with reviewing ingredient labels and maintaining records, but it does not replace supplier information, food safety procedures, staff training, or legal obligations.
